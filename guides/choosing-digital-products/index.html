A practical buying checklist

How to choose a digital product before you buy

Use this guide to check whether a bundle, course or eBook fits your goal. It does not replace the individual seller's listing or licence.

1. Start with the task, not the discount

Write down what you want to do: edit a short video, learn an animation workflow, understand YouTube SEO or read about a specific topic. Choose the relevant collection instead of buying only because a listing advertises a large number of files.

2. Inspect a representative preview

For video assets, check the actual clip quality, dimensions, audio and included formats. For a course, look for sample lessons and a syllabus. For an eBook bundle, ask for sample pages and a contents list. A cover image alone is not a complete preview.

3. Check the full cost and requirements

Confirm whether you need a specific device, editing application, paid subscription or reading app. A product price does not necessarily include third-party software. Use the current marketplace checkout price rather than an offer printed inside a thumbnail.

4. Separate usage rights from ownership

Buying a file does not automatically transfer copyright. Posting rights, commercial use, client work, redistribution and resale can be different permissions. Ask for written terms covering the underlying footage, music, images and other third-party material.

5. Read access and support terms

Check how the files or lessons are delivered, how long access lasts, and what the seller says about updates, support and refunds. If a term is missing, ask before payment rather than assuming it is included.

6. Be realistic about results

Reels do not guarantee views. SEO lessons do not guarantee rankings. Business courses do not guarantee income. Evaluate the material and the work you can put into it, not just outcome-focused wording in a title.

Questions to ask the seller

  • What exactly is included, and can I see a representative sample?
  • Which tools, versions and devices do I need?
  • What commercial, posting or resale rights are included?
  • Are there additional subscription costs?
  • What are the delivery, access, support and refund terms?
